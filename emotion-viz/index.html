COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS

These visualizations demonstrate how identical slot machine math can generate dramatically different emotional experiences depending on player profile, bankroll structure, and session duration.

The Casual Player exhibits sharp emotional spikes from relatively small wins, illustrating how novelty amplifies perceived reward value during short sessions. The Experienced Grinder shows emotional stabilization over time as repeated exposure reduces reactivity to common outcomes and reinforces expectation calibration.

The Unlucky Chaser demonstrates how extended dry spells combined with higher wagers create compounding frustration and emotional collapse. Even without changing the underlying reel math, bankroll pressure intensifies negative emotional response.

Together, these simulations show that slot design is not solely about payout percentages. Session pacing, volatility spacing, reward frequency, and bankroll depletion curves all contribute directly to emotional engagement and player retention behavior.
